Female Suffrage and Human Capital Accumulation

Francisca Kubista

This project studies whether female human capital contributed to the adoption of women’s suffrage across countries. It combines historical cross-country data on education, institutional change, and demographic dynamics.

Abstract

This paper investigates the relationship between female education and the timing of women’s suffrage. Using duration models and panel evidence, the analysis explores how human capital may have shaped political inclusion and institutional reform.

Keywords

Female suffrage; human capital; political economy; demographic transition; economic history.
